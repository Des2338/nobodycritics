One Battle After Another (2025)

⭐ 9.5/10

One Battle After Another (2025)

Genre: Comedy, Drama, Action

Runtime: 2 hr 42 min

Director: Paul Thomas Anderson

Initial Thoughts

The Oscars have finally come and gone, and to say that 2025 was a successful year for movies would be an understatement. With films like Sinners, Marty Supreme, Weapons, and Zootopia 2 all making noise, the competition was stacked. However, the film that stood above them all—and took home Best Picture—was One Battle After Another.

Performances

One thing about me is that I’m a huge Leonardo DiCaprio fan, and at this point, he just doesn’t miss. His performance as Bob Ferguson is emotionally grounded and serves as the heart of the film, as he tries to protect his daughter while being pulled back into his past.

The standout for me, though, was Benicio del Toro as Sergio St. Carlos. He’s calm, calculated, and always composed—playing the perfect “order” to Bob’s chaos.

I also have to give major credit to Sean Penn as Steven J. Lockjaw, a performance that earned him Best Supporting Actor. There’s something immediately unsettling about him, and his presence adds a layer of tension throughout the film—especially in scenes involving Bob’s daughter, Willa.

Direction & Tone

What really impressed me was how the film blends comedy, drama, action, and suspense without ever feeling forced. Everything flows naturally, which speaks to the strength of Paul Thomas Anderson’s direction. The film never feels like it’s trying too hard—it just works.

Story

The plot—a father forced to confront his past to save his daughter—may sound familiar, but the execution makes it feel fresh. The film stays engaging through strong character work and emotional stakes, making it more than just a typical action-drama.

Pros

  • Strong, layered performances across the board
  • Excellent character dynamics (Bob vs. Sergio especially)
  • Natural blending of multiple genres
  • Engaging, character-driven story
  • Well-deserved Best Picture winner

Cons

  • Pacing drags at times, especially with the long runtime
  • Could’ve explored Bob’s backstory (French 75) more in depth

Who Is This For?

  • Fans of Leonardo DiCaprio and strong performances
  • People who enjoy genre-blending films
  • Viewers who like emotional, character-driven stories
  • Fans of Paul Thomas Anderson

Who Might Skip It?

  • Viewers who prefer fast-paced, non-stop action
  • Anyone not into longer films
  • People who don’t enjoy slower storytelling

Rewatchability

Yes – There’s a lot to pick up on with performances and character dynamics.

Would I Recommend It?

Yes – Especially if you’re into strong acting and layered storytelling.

Final Thoughts

Even with a few minor pacing issues, One Battle After Another delivers where it matters most. The performances, characters, and storytelling all come together to create a film that feels both massive in scope and personal at the same time. In a stacked year of movies, it absolutely earns its place as Best Picture.

Rating

9.5/10
