One Battle After Another (2025)

Genre: Comedy, Drama, Action

Runtime: 2 hr 42 min

Director: Paul Thomas Anderson

Initial Thoughts

The Oscars have finally come and gone, and to say that 2025 was a successful year for movies would be an understatement. With films like Sinners, Marty Supreme, Weapons, and Zootopia 2 all making noise, the competition was stacked. However, the film that stood above them all—and took home Best Picture—was One Battle After Another.

Performances

Leonardo DiCaprio delivers a standout performance as Bob Ferguson, a father trying to protect his daughter while being pulled back into a past life he can’t escape. He brings emotional depth and acts as the heart of the film.

Benicio del Toro stands out as Sergio St. Carlos, the calm, collected counterbalance to Bob’s chaos. His presence is magnetic and keeps scenes grounded.

Sean Penn is chilling as the corrupt Steven J. Lockjaw. His mannerisms, voice, and the tension he creates immediately make you uneasy, especially in scenes with Bob’s daughter, Willa (Chase Infiniti). Penn fully deserves his Best Supporting Actor win.

Direction & Tone

Paul Thomas Anderson masterfully balances comedy, drama, action, and suspense. The film never feels forced or overstuffed; everything flows naturally, making the tonal shifts feel organic and enhancing the characters and story.

Story

While the plot—a father confronting his past to save his daughter—may sound familiar, the execution feels fresh. The character dynamics, emotional stakes, and suspense keep you engaged for the entire 2 hour 42 minutes. It’s familiar territory done exceptionally well.

Pros

  • Strong, layered performances
  • Excellent character dynamics (Bob vs. Sergio especially)
  • Natural blending of multiple genres
  • Engaging, character-driven story
  • Deserved Best Picture winner

Cons

  • Pacing drags in parts of the long runtime
  • Bob’s backstory (French 75) could use more exploration

Who Is This For?

  • Fans of Leonardo DiCaprio and strong acting
  • Viewers who enjoy genre-blending films
  • People who like emotionally grounded, character-driven stories
  • Fans of Paul Thomas Anderson

Who Might Skip It?

  • Viewers who prefer nonstop, fast-paced action
  • Those not into long films
  • People who dislike slower, character-focused storytelling

Rewatchability

Yes – There’s a lot to notice with performances, character dynamics, and subtle moments throughout the film.

Would I Recommend It?

Yes – The acting, story, and genre balance make this a must-watch.

Final Thoughts

Despite minor pacing issues, One Battle After Another succeeds in its characters, story, and performances. It’s both massive and personal, and fully deserves its Best Picture win.

Rating

9.5/10
